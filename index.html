Privacy Policy

The Night Shift Desk: Night Startpage (browser extension and homepage). Last updated: 12 November 2026.

Summary

The extension does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your settings, quick links and tasks are saved only in your own browser (local storage). We cannot see them. If you clear your browser data or remove the extension, they are deleted. The optional backup file is created on your computer and is never uploaded anywhere.

Website icons

The extension uses the browser's built-in favicon feature to show the icon of each quick link you add. This happens inside your browser.

Searches and links

When you search or open a link, your browser goes straight to the search engine or website you chose (Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing or your own links). Their own privacy policies apply to what happens there. We receive nothing.

Permissions

The extension requests only the "favicon" permission, used as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
